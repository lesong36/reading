Weather scientists collect information about the weather. They watch how the air moves. They watch changes in temperature and moisture.

This information goes to supercomputers. They run complicated models of the weather. This shows the weather forecast.

The system is not perfect. Scientists can’t always get good information. But weather models are getting better all the time.
