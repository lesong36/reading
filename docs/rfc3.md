## Unit 6 Laser Eye Surgery

Many people wear glasses or contact lenses. But more and more people are getting laser eye surgery. This is a surgery using lasers to make a person's eyesight better. LASIK is the most popular. First, the eye surgeon cuts a part of the eye. Then, they fold that part back. Next, they use a laser to change the shape inside the eye. This helps light get into the eye better. It also helps the eye focus better. This means that the person will be able to see better. Then, the folded part is put back and the eye can heal. The surgery is usually completed in fifteen to thirty minutes. After the surgery, the person can see but will need to rest. Their eyesight gets better each day. Most people will have good eyesight for a long time.

## Unit 14 The Meaning of Zero

Zero is a special number. It means "nothing" on its own. But when it is used with other numbers, zero is important. It can change the meaning of other numbers. You can add (+) zero to a number or subtract (–) zero from a number. Then that other number remains unchanged. Only zero can do this. You can multiply (x) a number by zero. Then that number also becomes zero. Only zero can do this, too. But you cannot divide (÷) a number by zero. Even though zero means "nothing," it is needed to show how big a number is. The number 607 could be mistaken for 67 without the zero. Zero also shows us how small a number is. For example, 0.002 is a very small number. Zero means "nothing" on its own, but the meaning of zero is important.
